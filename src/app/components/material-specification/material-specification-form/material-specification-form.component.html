Min / Max Limit Equation
Set Min/Max limit by calculation from other parameters. Click a field, then insert tokens.
Tokens: C = measured value · specMax(C)/specMin(C) = that parameter's spec limit · IF(cond, a, b), MIN(...), MAX(...).
Example — "per 0.01% C below its max, +0.06% Mn above its max, up to 1.65%":
MIN(1.65, specMax(Mn) + 6 * MAX(0, specMax(C) - C))
