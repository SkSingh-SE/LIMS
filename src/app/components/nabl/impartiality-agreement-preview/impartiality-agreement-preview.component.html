I, {{ employee.name }}, holding the position of {{ employee.designationName }} at Divine Metallurgical Services Pvt. Ltd., do hereby solemnly declare and agree to the following:

1. IMPARTIALITY COMMITMENT

I am aware that Divine Metallurgical Services Pvt. Ltd. (DMSPL) is committed to ensure impartiality in its laboratory activities. DMSPL laboratory activities are structured and managed so as to safeguard impartiality.

I commit myself to carry out all laboratory activities impartially and not to allow commercial, financial or other pressures to compromise impartiality. I will identify risks to my impartiality on an on-going basis. This shall include those risks that arise from my activities, or from my relationships, or from the relationships of DMSPL personnel.

If a risk to impartiality is identified, I shall immediately inform the Quality Manager/Director to enable the laboratory to demonstrate how it eliminates or minimizes such risk.

2. CONFIDENTIALITY COMMITMENT

I am responsible, through legally enforceable commitments, for the management of all information obtained or created during the performance of laboratory activities. I shall inform the customer in advance, of the information it intends to place in the public domain. Except for information that the customer makes publicly available, or when agreed between the laboratory and the customer (e.g. for the purpose of responding to complaints), all other information is considered proprietary information and shall be regarded as confidential.

When the laboratory is required by law or authorized by contractual arrangements to release confidential information, the customer or individual concerned shall, unless prohibited by law, be notified of the information provided.

Information about the customer obtained from sources other than the customer (e.g. complainant, regulators) shall be confidential between the customer and the laboratory. The provider (source) of this information shall be confidential to the laboratory and shall not be shared with the customer, unless agreed by the source.

3. CONFLICT OF INTEREST

I declare that I have no personal, financial or other interest which might influence my judgment in performing laboratory activities. I will not engage in any activity that may conflict with the interests of DMSPL or its customers.
